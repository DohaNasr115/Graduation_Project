# Graduation_Project
## project objective
- Developed a Management System for Nuclear Medicine Hospital, Asyut, Egypt
 Designed and implemented a hospital management system to replace the traditional paper-based system, streamlining administrative and operational workflows.
 Collaborated with the medical and administrative teams to ensure their needs were met and provided tailored technical solutions.
- Conducted hands-on training sessions for staff on the new system, facilitating quick adoption and streaming daily operations.
- Improved internal processes and administrative procedures, leading to a 30% increase in operational efficiency.
